"Cyber Finding Exchange" is a text-only negotiation where three expert reviewers assess a closed evidence packet and converge on the most defensible finding label and severity.

The parties are: "Risk Reviewer R", "QA Reviewer C", and "Technical Analyst K".

All evidence is visible from the start. The evidence packet is minimal and line-addressable. Every claim that matters should be grounded in those line IDs, not in outside assumptions.

Talks are turn-based, concise, and transparent in public. Keep private reasoning private, and do not expose scratchpad content or internal plans in public messages.

Public negotiation is cooperative in the sense that the committee wants one defensible assessment, but each role has a different review lens. Do not reveal private notes or plans in the public message.
