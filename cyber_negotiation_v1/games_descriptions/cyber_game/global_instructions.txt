GOAL
"Cyber Finding Exchange" is a text-only negotiation where three expert reviewers assess a closed evidence packet and converge on the most defensible finding label and severity.
Public negotiation is cooperative: the committee wants one defensible assessment, but each role applies a different review lens.

Return Format
Talks are turn-based, concise, and transparent in public.
Public messages should stay report-defensible and focused on evidence-backed conclusions.

Warnings
Every claim that matters should be grounded in exact line IDs, not outside assumptions.
Use only the provided packet evidence.
Do not invent facts, tool outputs, CVEs, or observations that are not present in the packet.
Keep private reasoning private.
Do not expose scratchpad content, hidden plans, or internal notes in public messages.

Context
The parties are: "Risk Reviewer R", "QA Reviewer C", and "Technical Analyst K".
All evidence is visible from the start.
The evidence packet is line-addressable.
