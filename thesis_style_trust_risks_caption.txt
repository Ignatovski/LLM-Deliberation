Figure X: Trust risks in multi-agent LLM deliberation across domains. Panel A shows polynomial game trust risks: success rate drop (0.28) calculated as the difference between baseline cooperative success rate (77.7%, n=269 GPT-5 + Claude runs) and obstructive adversarial success rate (49.3%, n=270 runs); utility loss (0.76) calculated as the relative reduction in average utility for Claude cooperative agents (Analyst A, Builder B, Critic C) between baseline cooperative conditions (mean utility=3.01, n=135 Claude runs) and targeted adversarial manipulation (mean utility=0.73, n=135 Claude runs). Panel B shows cybersecurity trust risks: wrong consensus rate (0.49) representing the proportion of committees reaching incorrect consensus across conditions C3-C7 (n=35 runs); under-severity rate (0.43) representing the proportion of severity assessments that underestimate threat severity across the same conditions. Error bars are not shown as these represent aggregate effect sizes across experimental conditions.
